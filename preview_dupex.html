FEATURES

One keybind, whole inventory doubles at once. No menus, no cooldown, no faffing around.

Duplicates anything — items, blocks, shulker boxes, even stacks of enchanted gear.

Client side only. Your server does not need the mod installed to work with it.

IN THE JAR

DupeX lets you duplicate any stack of items with a single hotkey. Hold the key, every stack doubles. Works in singleplayer and on servers where you have operator permissions. Lightweight, zero bloat, no dependencies beyond Fabric API. Config file generates on first launch — rebind the key, enable or disable the whole mod, all in one json.
